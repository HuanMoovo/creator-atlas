## What is this

Creator Atlas is a Chinese-first, open-source knowledge base for video creators. It breaks "making videos" into seven method domains — positioning and topics, scripting and storytelling, production and shooting, editing and post-production, packaging and distribution, growth and analytics, monetization and business — and condenses dozens of content types into 8 production methods. Every external link is verified one by one, and platform-rule content carries the date it was last checked.

The story and organizing principles behind the project are in the [Preface](docs/preface.md). The English edition is rolling out: translated pages serve in English, and everything else falls back to the Chinese original.
