# Creator Atlas · Production Methods

> Organized not by subject matter but by "how it is made": every content type is consolidated into 8 production methods. Each method distills its shared theory and methodology first, then details how individual content types vary within it, with a complete workflow.
> Coverage: talking-head opinion, explainers, vlogs, food, scripted, tutorials, interviews, animation, film recaps, commercials and 70+ content formats in total, all folded into the 8 production methods.

## The Eight Production Methods

| Method | Representative Types | Methodological Core | Workflow Skeleton |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Talking to Camera](talking/README.md) | opinion, explainers, commentary, niches, talks | Personal trust & information efficiency | Thesis → Script → Preparation → Shooting → Rough cut → Fine cut → Publish |
| [Real-Life & Documentary](documentary/README.md) | vlogs, travel, food, shop visits, pets | Authenticity & companionship | Promise → Plan → Field capture → Organization → Structure → Fine cut → Publish |
| [Scripted & Performance](scripted/README.md) | comedy, micro-dramas, shorts, music, dance | Choreographed conflict & emotion | Script → Rehearsal → Production → Shooting → Test screening → Polish → Serialize |
| [Demo & Screencast](demo/README.md) | tutorials, gaming, reviews, teardowns, follow-alongs | Reproducibility & information design | Goal → Flow design → Recording → Speed & structure → Reproducibility check → Publish |
| [Conversation & Interview](conversation/README.md) | interviews, podcasts, roundtables, live chats | The structure of asking & listening | Guest → Question list → Setup → Recording → Distillation → Clips → Reuse |
| [Animation & Synthesis](animated/README.md) | animation, whiteboard, AI-generated, faceless | Constructed imagery instead of shooting | Voice → Visual plan → Assets → Animation → Sound sync → Review → Template |
| [Editorial & Remix](editorial/README.md) | film recaps, mashups, meme edits, reactions | Re-narrating existing footage | Survey → Script → Collect & tag → Re-edit → Voice-over → Package → Compliance check |
| [Commercial Production](commercial/README.md) | ads, brand films, launches, e-commerce | Goal-driven delivery standards | Brief → Proposal → Production → Shooting → Revision rounds → Delivery → Reuse |

Each method page contains five sections: applicable types in detail, key principles, the standard workflow (seven phases with actions, outputs and checkpoints), workflow variants by type, and common pitfalls.

## Classification Rules

- Vertical short-form and horizontal long-form are distribution formats that can appear in any method; the general methods of distribution live in [Packaging & Distribution](../methods/packaging.md).
- Livestreams, recorded courses and other real-time formats classify by primary scene: talking-led into Talking to Camera, conversation-led into Conversation & Interview, demo-led into Demo & Screencast.
- Hybrid formats follow the primary scene: one piece belongs to exactly one method, and the secondary format is handled as a variation note on that page — a demo inside a talking-head video, an interview inside a documentary.
- Edge cases: sports highlights, gameplay edits and film mashups all go to Editorial & Remix; wedding and event records split between personal documentary and Commercial Production depending on whether they are delivered to a client; reality shows go to Scripted & Performance or Real-Life & Documentary depending on how scripted they are.

## Why Organize by Production Method

One production method shares its workflow, skill stack and evaluation metrics; subject matter goes stale, methods stay stable. Learning by method lets one methodology migrate across many types; listing by type makes dozens of entries repeat the same thing. This is why the handbook keeps only 8 pages here.

## Roadmap

New content formats merge into the existing 8 methods; a brand-new production method (interactive video, say) gets a new page. See the [Content Roadmap](../meta/roadmap.md).
