# Creator Atlas · Method Domains

> The seven method domains are arranged in real production order, forming the full chain from idea to income. Every page shares a seven-section structure whose "acceptance criteria" are the passing line; the companion [Production Methods](../genres/README.md) pages give the do's and don'ts for each content format.

## The Creation Chain

| Order | Method Domain | In One Line | Main Handoffs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | [Positioning & Topic Selection](positioning.md) | Decide what to make and for whom | One-line positioning, topic bank |
| 2 | [Script & Storytelling](script.md) | Turn ideas into structure and lines | Script, storyboard |
| 3 | [Production & Shooting](production.md) | Turn the script into usable footage | Footage, B-roll |
| 4 | [Editing & Post-Production](editing.md) | Turn footage into a finished video | Finished cut, subtitles |
| 5 | [Packaging & Distribution](packaging.md) | Get the work clicked and seen | Title, thumbnail, publishing |
| 6 | [Growth & Analytics](growth.md) | Find problems in data and iterate | Retro records, experiment conclusions |
| 7 | [Monetization & Business](monetization.md) | Make creating sustainable | Income structure, rates |

## How to Use

- **New project**: read from page 1 in order; enter the next stage only after passing that page's "acceptance criteria".
- **Existing channel**: audit yourself against each page's "common mistakes" list and fill what is missing.
- **Teams**: map method domains to roles and duties via [Roles & Skills](../start/roles-skills.md).
