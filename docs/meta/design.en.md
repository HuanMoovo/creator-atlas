# Creator Atlas · About This Repo

> Creator Atlas (a panorama handbook of video creation methods) is a Chinese-first, structured, open-source knowledge base for video creators: "making videos" broken into a complete chain of methods from positioning and topics to monetization and business, with every content type integrated into 8 production methods.
> The name comes from creator and atlas: drawing as complete a map as possible for "making videos."
> This page is the repository overview: positioning, content map, quality standards, layout and how to take part.

---

## 01 What this is

- **An executable creation chain**: knowledge arranged in the order "positioning → topics → script → shooting → editing → packaging & distribution → growth → monetization", with methods, steps and acceptance bars at every stage.
- **Chinese-first, two platform lines**: Simplified Chinese is the primary language; international platforms (YouTube, TikTok) and domestic platforms (Bilibili, Douyin, WeChat Channels, Xiaohongshu) are both covered.
- **Integrated coverage**: dozens of content types are integrated into 8 production-method handbooks, from talking to camera to commercial production.
- **Continuously maintained**: platform mechanics and fees carry their last-checked date; external links are verified one by one and re-checked by CI.

Who it is for:

- People who want to start: walk from Getting Started to publishing your first video.
- Creators whose growth has stalled: use the Method Domains as a diagnostic checklist and inspect every stage.
- Teams and studios: build production workflows with the Roles & Skills Map and the Checklists & Templates.
- Contributors: extend, proofread and translate following the shared templates.

Out of scope: hot-news coverage, zero-basics tutorials for a single piece of software, paid-course funnels.

## 03 Quality standards

- **Single source of truth**: when content exists elsewhere, reference it instead of copying; the cross-reference format is《Handbook name》§X.
- **Verifiable**: links are checked one by one; platform rules, fees and thresholds carry their last-checked date (confirm against the latest official documentation before acting).
- **Writing rules**: Simplified Chinese; unified terminology (see [GLOSSARY.md](../../GLOSSARY.md)); no unverified promises or statements no one can act on.
- **AI usage**: AI output must be verified by a human; AI-generated assets are disclosed as platforms require.
- **Link policy**: only reachable entries of real value to creators; for each category, only the most commonly used one.
- **No empty talk**: every recommendation maps to an action; figures come with ranges and definitions, not single absolute values.
