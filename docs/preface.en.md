# Preface · Why Creator Atlas Exists

There is no shortage of single-point tips for making videos. What has been missing is the skeleton that organizes them, and one complete route from "I want to make videos" to "producing consistently."

This repository addresses four long-standing problems:

- **Fragmented, often mystified methods**: most content stops at single-point tips (camera settings, editing shortcuts, growth folklore) without a system. The atlas breaks creation into seven method domains, each with methods, steps and acceptance bars.
- **Broken links between stages**: positioning, topics, scripting, shooting, editing, packaging, growth and monetization all constrain each other; one weak stage drags the whole down. The repository lays out knowledge in the order real creation happens and spells out what each stage hands to the next.
- **Opaque platform mechanics**: algorithm rumors abound and rules keep changing. The repository cites official documentation and verifiable sources only, marks the date each fact was checked, verifies every external link one by one, and has CI re-check them.
- **AI is rewriting production**: AI tools are changing how scripts, voice-overs, editing and assets get made. The repository collects usable workflows and disclosure boundaries, while keeping the emphasis on human judgment.

The name comes from creator and atlas: drawing as complete a map as possible for "making videos." There is one organizing principle: lay out knowledge in the order real creation happens. The content is Chinese-first, covering both international and domestic platforms; every document answers one concrete question, and documents cross-reference instead of repeating.

This is a continuously maintained open-source project, and mistakes are unavoidable: Issues and PRs with corrections or additions are welcome, and errata get the highest priority.
